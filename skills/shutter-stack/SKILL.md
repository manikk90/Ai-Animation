# Shutter stack

Photo cards that drop in on a camera flash, stacking up.

**Input:** a 9:16 talking-head clip.
**Run it:** `/shutter-stack` and attach or name the file.

## Steps

1. Take the photos I attach (or frames from my clip).
2. On each beat, flash white and drop a polaroid-style card at a slight random angle.
3. Stack the cards on top of each other with soft shadows.
4. Add a shutter sound on every drop.

- Transcribe my audio with word-level timings, so every graphic lands on the word I say.

- Render a 1080×1920 MP4 at 30 fps with my original audio. Never re-grade or filter my face unless the skill says so.
