# Match cut

The same word on a new page, landing in exactly the same spot on screen.

**Input:** a 9:16 talking-head clip.
**Run it:** `/match-cut` and attach or name the file.

## Steps

1. Find the repeated keyword in my script.
2. For each repeat, show a different background (web page, document, poster) with that word in it.
3. Line every version of the word up to the same position and size, so the cut feels seamless.
4. Cut on the beat of my voice.

## Every time it runs

- Transcribe my audio with word-level timings, so every graphic lands on the word I say.
- Keep text out of the top 250 px and the bottom 420 px (Instagram buttons and caption).
- Render a 1080×1920 MP4 at 30 fps with my original audio. Never re-grade or filter my face unless the skill says so.
- Show me 3 snapshots before the final render, and ask before overwriting files.
