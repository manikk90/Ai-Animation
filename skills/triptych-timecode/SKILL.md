# Triptych timecode

Three clips side by side, each with its own live timecode.

**Input:** three clips.
**Run it:** `/triptych-timecode` and attach or name the file.

## Steps

1. Arrange three clips in three vertical panels.
2. Run a separate timecode on each panel.
3. Highlight the panel that's speaking.
4. Sync the three clips by their audio.
