# Particle text

Words built out of falling dots that assemble on the beat.

**Input:** a 9:16 talking-head clip.
**Run it:** `/particle-text` and attach or name the file.

## Steps

1. Pick a key word or short phrase.
2. Scatter particles across the frame, then pull them into the letter shapes.
3. Hold the word, then blow it apart on the next beat.
4. Match the particle colour to my brand colours.
