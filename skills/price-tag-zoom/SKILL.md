# Price-tag zoom

A slow push-in on a product with a floating price tag.

**Input:** a 9:16 talking-head clip.
**Run it:** `/price-tag-zoom` and attach or name the file.

2. Float a price tag card that swings in when I say the price.
3. Strike through the old price if I mention a discount.
4. Add a soft 'cha-ching' sound.
