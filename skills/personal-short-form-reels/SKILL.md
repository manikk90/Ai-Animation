# Personal short-form reel

A fast personal reel with my own B-roll and word-by-word captions.

**Input:** a 9:16 talking-head clip.
**Run it:** `/personal-short-form-reels` and attach or name the file.

## Steps

1. Cut silences and filler words so the pacing stays tight.
2. Every 2–3 seconds, cut away to a matching clip from my B-roll folder (search by what I'm saying).
3. Add 2–3 word captions with the current word highlighted.
4. Add a punch-in zoom on key words.

## Every time it runs

- Transcribe my audio with word-level timings, so every graphic lands on the word I say.
- Keep text out of the top 250 px and the bottom 420 px (Instagram buttons and caption).
- Render a 1080×1920 MP4 at 30 fps with my original audio. Never re-grade or filter my face unless the skill says so.
- Show me 3 snapshots before the final render, and ask before overwriting files.
